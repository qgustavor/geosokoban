# Country-curiosity sources

These references support the short, original dialogue in `src/content/story-facts.json`. The lines are not quotations or translated excerpts. They introduce one detail about a country whose flag appears on the completed board; they do not claim the puzzle depicts the landmark itself.

The sources were consulted on 21 November 2026. Specific populations, conservation rankings, current counts and superlatives are deliberately omitted. Recheck facts and translations as part of educational review when changing the content.
